|---|------|-----------|---------|
| 1 | Landing page | "Drop your tradebook" button and the line "Files are processed on your device and never uploaded" | 3 |
| 2 | Pick the file | The synthetic CSV being chosen, then the progress bar | 3 |
| 3 | The number | Net P&L after charges, trades, traded value | 3 |
| 4 | Where the money went | Charges as a share of gross profit, marked "estimated" | 3 |
| 5 | Revenge trades | The count and their combined P&L, labelled "before charges" | 3 |
| 6 | Summary | Three headline stats, the Samvat year, the URL and the disclaimer | 3 |
| 7 | Share | Tap Share; the system share sheet opens with the image | 2 |
